### Featured projects

#### 💊 [Hay Fever Prescribing Forecast](https://github.com/MSyukri420/HayFever_Forecast)
Forecasts prescriptions three months ahead for 936 GP practices, from NHS open data and weather, with a Power BI report.
*2.9% monthly error on 30 unseen months, against 3.9% for the best baseline.*

`Python` `DuckDB` `LightGBM` `PyTorch` `Optuna` `FastAPI` `Docker` `Power BI` `Time Series`

---

#### 💳 [Thin-File Borrowers](https://github.com/MSyukri420/Thin-File-Borrowers)
Scores loan risk for applicants with no credit bureau record, built on 307,511 Home Credit applications.
*AUC 0.785 for thin-file applicants on a locked test set.*

`Python` `pandas` `scikit-learn` `LightGBM` `Optuna` `Calibration` `Fairness` `Credit Risk`

---

#### ⚽ [Corner Chance Planner](https://github.com/MSyukri420/final-project-apv)
Decision support for attacking corners, built on 879 real corners (StatsBomb event data and 360 freeze frames), with a web app for coaches.
*Predicts who wins the first ball at AUC 0.68 on unseen competitions.*

`Python` `scikit-learn` `SHAP` `FastAPI` `StatsBomb` `Sports Analytics`

---

#### 🧤 [Serie A Clean Sheet Prediction](https://github.com/MSyukri420/CleanSheet_Prediction)
Forecasts clean sheets from each team's last five matches, built with PySpark.
*"High" readiness teams kept clean sheets 2.5× more often than "Low" on two unseen seasons.*

`PySpark` `Spark MLlib` `pandas` `Sports Analytics`

---

#### 🏢 [CCK HR Performance System](https://github.com/MSyukri420/cck-syukri)
A full-stack appraisal and survey app built for a real company.
*Deployed with one Docker command.*

`React` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `Docker` `Full-Stack`
